How I work

A short, boring process, which is the point.

  1. You send the footage and the goal. Raw files, any format, plus one line on what the video is for — launch, awareness, retargeting, organic. If there’s a reference you like, send that too.

  2. First cut within 24-48hrs. Fully finished, not a rough pass — captions, sound, colour, the lot. You should be able to judge it the way your audience would.

  3. Two rounds of revisions, then delivery. You get the export in every ratio you need, plus the project file on request. If something isn’t working I’ll say so rather than quietly do it.

Beauty is my edge.

I started as a photographer, spent over a decade training beauty professionals, and now bring both into editing. I know how a technique should look on screen, where a viewer needs a close-up, and what makes a tutorial easy to follow.

I shoot, cut, grade and caption my own footage, so a brief goes from idea to finished reel with one person.

FAQ

What’s your turnaround time?

48-60hrs per short-form edit. Rush available for an added fee.

What are your rates?

Per-edit or monthly retainer, depending on volume. Send me the brief and I’ll quote.

Do you offer revisions?

Two rounds included. Beyond that I bill hourly, though it rarely comes up if the brief is clear.

Do you only work with beauty and travel brands?

Not at all. I work with any brand or niche. Beauty and travel are just where I know the audience from the inside, so I can add extra value there.

Can you write the captions and hooks too?

Yes. English, Hindi, Bengali and Hinglish. I write and time them myself rather than auto-generating.
